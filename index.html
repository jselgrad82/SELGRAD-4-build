Three Adobe Tools, Three Different Jobs

  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe InDesign

Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign are all part of Adobe Creative Cloud, but each is built for a different type of design work. Here’s a quick look at what each tool does best.

Adobe Photoshop

Adobe Photoshop is a powerful image editing software used by photographers, graphic designers, and digital artists. It allows users to manipulate photos, create digital artwork, and design graphics for web and print.

Adobe Illustrator

Adobe Illustrator is a vector graphics editor used by illustrators, graphic designers, and logo designers. It enables users to create scalable vector artwork, illustrations, and typography for various media.

Adobe InDesign

Adobe InDesign is a desktop publishing software used by graphic designers, editors, and layout artists. It enables users to create layouts for print and digital media, including magazines, brochures, and eBooks.

Quick Facts

  • Photoshop: Best for photo editing and digital art.
  • Illustrator: Best for vector graphics and illustrations.
  • InDesign: Best for layouts and publishing.
